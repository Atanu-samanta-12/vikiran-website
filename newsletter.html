About the Team

The Vikiran Newsletter Team is a group of students working together to contribute to Confluence Chronicles, a collaborative science newsletter connecting student communities across institutions.

From exploring scientific ideas and writing articles to editing and presenting content, the team works together to bring interesting physics and scientific perspectives to the wider community.
